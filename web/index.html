YT Transcriber

The transcript of the YouTube video you're watching, with clickable timestamps, search, copy and download. It runs inside the YouTube page in your browser, using YouTube's own captions. Nothing is sent anywhere except YouTube.

2. Add it on iPad or iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap Copy bookmarklet above.
  2. Bookmark this page: Share button → Add Bookmark → Save.
  3. Open Bookmarks (the book icon) → Edit → tap the new bookmark.
  4. Rename it YT Transcript, clear the address, paste the copied code, then tap Done.

3. Use it

  1. Open a video on youtube.com in Safari (not the YouTube app).
  2. Open Bookmarks and tap YT Transcript. The transcript appears at the top right of the page.
  3. Tap a timestamp to jump there. Tap the bookmark again to hide or show the panel.

If nothing happens, let the video play for a second and tap it again. Updates to this site reach the bookmarklet automatically; there's nothing to reinstall.
